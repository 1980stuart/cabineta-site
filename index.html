cabineta.

Your digital cabinet of curiosities – from found to sold.

cabineta is a phone app for people who buy and resell vintage and antique pieces. It keeps a record of every piece from the day it is found to the day it sells, and shows the profit on each one.

What it does

  • Stock. Photograph a piece at the shop, note what was paid and where, and it is in the list. Search it, see what is unlisted, listed and sold.
  • Profit on every sale. Sale price and postage charged, less what was paid, fees, refunds, postage and packaging.
  • Sales that fill themselves in. A seller can connect their own shop, and the app reads its listings and orders so sales are matched to the pieces they were.
  • A short list of what needs attention. Only the things the seller has to decide.

Connecting an Etsy shop

A seller connects their own Etsy shop through Etsy's sign-in and approves what the app may do. cabineta never sees the seller's Etsy password.

It reads that shop's listings, orders, fees and refunds. When the seller asks, it can save a draft listing in their shop for them to check and publish on Etsy themselves. It never publishes, changes or removes a live listing, does not message buyers, and does not use buyer email addresses. It reaches Etsy only through Etsy's own API. A seller can disconnect at any time.

Where it is up to

cabineta is in development and is being tested with one vintage shop in Australia. It is not yet open to the public.
